06 — DEFENSE
Defense in depth

Why a karma score
is hard to fake.

Receipt gating
No attestation without a verified on-chain payment. Spam costs real USDC, per attempt, forever.
Counterparty diversity
Same-cluster reviews get discounted. One friendly wallet doesn't make a crowd — and a tight cluster of three doesn't either.
Temporal decay
Recent behavior weighs more than historical. Old scores fade. Liveness feeds directly into Provider Karma.
Attester diversity
Small groups hyping the same agent see diminishing returns on every additional attestation. Cabals are visible, not rewarded.
